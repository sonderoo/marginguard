FAQ

Common Questions

Does MarginGuard work on Drafts before I publish?

Yes. MarginGuard works on both Active Listings and Manage Drafts in eBay Seller Hub, so you can catch low-margin items before they ever go live.

Is my eBay store data or Item Cost sent to a server?

No. Everything runs locally inside your Chrome browser. Your listing titles, prices, and costs are saved strictly in Chrome's local storage and are never shared or sold.

Why use this instead of a spreadsheet or AI chat?

MarginGuard reads your live eBay page in one second, remembers your costs permanently, and applies exact eBay fee formulas (including Store tiers, Top Rated discounts, and ad rates) without copy-pasting or math errors.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription in one click anytime through your Polar.sh customer portal.

Privacy Policy

MarginGuard runs locally inside your Chrome browser. Your eBay listing titles, prices, Item Costs, and shipping costs are stored strictly in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never sold, shared, or sent to external servers. Pro license key validation is handled securely through Polar.sh.
