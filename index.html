FAQ

Everything You Need to Know

Straight answers about account safety, fee accuracy, privacy, and billing.

Is MarginGuard safe for my eBay account? Does it need my login?

100% safe. MarginGuard never asks for your eBay password, API keys, or account permissions. It is a read-only Chrome extension that simply reads the prices and ad rates already visible on your screen in Seller Hub—just like a built-in calculator. It never modifies your listings or automates actions on your account.

How accurate is the fee math—and what happens when eBay changes fees?

MarginGuard calculates the full eBay fee stack: Standard (13.6%) or eBay Store (12.7%) Final Value Fees, Top Rated Seller discounts (-10%), International Buyer fees (+1.65%), Promoted Listings ad rates, and per-order fees ($0.30 under $10 / $0.40 over $10). Whenever eBay updates its fee schedule, just click Update Rates inside the extension to pull the latest rates instantly.

Does it work on Drafts before I publish, or only Active Listings?

Both. MarginGuard runs on Active Listings and Manage Drafts inside eBay Seller Hub. You can audit existing inventory to stop ongoing profit leaks, and check new drafts before they go live so you never list an underpriced item again.

How does "Lowest Offer to Accept" help with Best Offers?

When a buyer sends a Best Offer, it's easy to accidentally accept a price that leaves you with $1 in profit after Promoted Listing ads and shipping. MarginGuard calculates your exact floor price in advance—showing you the lowest dollar amount (and max % discount) you can accept while still hitting your Target Profit.

Are my winning products, prices, or Item Costs sent to your servers?

Never. We don't even have a database for your store data. Every listing title, price, Item Cost, and shipping cost stays 100% local inside your own Chrome browser (chrome.storage.local). The only external request MarginGuard makes is validating your optional Pro license key through Polar.sh.

What if I have hundreds of listings? Do I have to type every cost manually?

No. You can type Item Cost and My Shipping directly on any card (and MarginGuard remembers them permanently), or use Upload Costs (CSV) in Pro to import your entire inventory's costs from a spreadsheet in two seconds.

Can I use MarginGuard Pro on both my laptop and desktop?

Yes. Once you activate your Pro license key, it syncs automatically across your own Chrome devices signed into the same Google profile—no need to buy a second subscription.

Can I try it for free first, and how do I cancel Pro?

Yes—the Free plan works forever on up to 10 listings at a time, including live inline Seller Hub badges and 1 free Suggested Price / Lowest Offer preview. If you upgrade to Pro ($19/month), you can manage or cancel your subscription in one click anytime directly from the extension or the Polar Customer Portal—no emails or hoops required.
